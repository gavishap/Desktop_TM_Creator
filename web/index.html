iCloud Emails

Mints Hide My Email aliases from your Apple ID (needs iCloud+). Each one is added to Accounts straight away with a name, address and a phone number from the pool, so it is ready to run. The pool below is the record of what was made and which addresses ended up as verified accounts.

Apple ID

Use the normal Apple ID password you sign in to icloud.com with. App-specific passwords do not work here — those are only for mail apps, and this app never reads iCloud mailboxes.

The password stays on this computer. When Apple asks for a code, a box pops up — type the code from your Apple device.

Gmail Emails

Makes brand-new addresses by inserting a period into one of your base Gmails (Gmail ignores dots, so mail still arrives in the same inbox). It remembers every variation it has already used and only makes fresh ones. Each gets a name, and a phone number is pulled from the pool automatically if one is free.

Credentials file

Your team's logins live in the project folder this app was shipped in. Pick your team's file to load the email inboxes, Apple IDs and Jivetel login in one go.

Google Sheet

Accounts that finish, and every iCloud alias made here, are copied into your team's sheet on their own — verified rows onto the accounts tab, aliases onto pool_emails. It happens in the background every minute or two; nothing is ever read back from the sheet, and rows still waiting are left out on purpose.

Email inboxes

The Gmail accounts the verification codes arrive in — either the account itself or the inbox its iCloud alias forwards to. Use a Gmail app password, not the normal one. iCloud mailboxes can't be read.

SMS (Jivetel)

Enter your Jivetel portal login. The app logs in and reads the Ticketmaster SMS code by phone number. Leave blank to type SMS codes by hand.
